## Optional verification interview

- CAN-19 (simulated), `/#/aplikuj/:slug`. The form offers a local interview after the profile CV. Starting and confirming turns saves the current application text and per-candidate/job transcript. The conversation always asks five basic questions about practical AI work: checking feasibility, personal model instructions/configuration, selecting model context, evaluating outputs and correcting failures. Rules inspect the complete stored CV and every earlier reply. Recognized details already in the CV are excluded from questions, and replies can resolve multiple areas. A resolved basic topic is replaced with another practical AI question, so a rich answer never reduces the total below five basics. Partial descriptions lead to one missing-facet question. Neither CV headlines nor business outcomes establish practical AI coverage. The assistant chooses zero to three optional clarifications only for unresolved relevant details after the five basics; it never fills a clarification quota. The total is five to eight questions, without repeating an already asked prompt. Older drafts, including short completed-but-unsubmitted conversations, switch to the current policy on resume; previously submitted reports retain their original sequence. Skipped questions count as asked and remain visible in the report. Skips and pauses are supported. The candidate opts in to attaching a completed current-source report before submitting. Changing the CV or offer requires restarting to attach it. Older version-1 drafts and applications remain usable.
- EMP-11 (simulated), `/#/app/kandydaci/:id`. The employer report tab reads a versioned snapshot of interview sources and answers attached at submission. It shows each saved question above its verbatim answer, followed by one short plain-language interpretation and its limits. Sources and detailed job relevance are expandable. Adaptive reports list all five areas, their recognized excerpts across replies, and any unrecognized details for expert review; this rule-based recognition is not proof of competence. The role-expert review section/form is removed; existing review metadata is retained only for save/import compatibility. New turns retain the exact question asked; legacy turns retain their original wording when displayed. The original evidence remains intact. An optional test is handled separately by EMP-12/CAN-20; there is no hiring recommendation or external AI service.
- EMP-12 (simulated), `/#/app/kandydaci/:id`. Immediately after interview replies, the report shows a test recommendation or explains why no grounded question was found. A finite local catalog proposes up to five A–D questions only for recognized, role-relevant gaps; the current catalog contains four topics and never fills a quota. The recruiter sees questions/options/reasons/limits/source quotes before invitation, can exclude questions, and can inspect/edit a separate key and question text. The recruiter decides directly after preview/selection; no expert name, review time or quality-confirmation checkbox is required. Invitation still requires 1–5 valid questions and an explicit recruiter action. No decision or continuing without a test sends nothing. A recruiter-selected question snapshot and local invitation are persisted separately from interview evidence and recruitment stage; controls remain accessible on a completed application. No email is sent. The reviewer can invalidate a question with a reason without rewriting its original key.
- CAN-20 (simulated), `/#/app/aplikacje/:id`. A separately approved test invitation appears in the candidate record and is announced in the applications list. Answers persist after each selection; candidates can leave questions unanswered, submit or decline. The candidate UI omits the key until submission; role simulation remains non-secure browser-local state. Results append to the recruiter report: correct/answered valid questions, total asked, valid unanswered and invalid counts. No assessed answers means no result; refusal remains distinct. Scores concern only that set and never rank candidates. Source and regression coverage: `optional-test.ts`, `optional-test-panel.tsx`, `optional-test.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/optional-test.spec.ts`.

The current CAN-19 policy asks Anna about model instructions for missing information, not project scope or the 30% time reduction. Replacement basics use a finite catalog of further AI details checked against the CV and previous replies. If those details are also recognized, an explicitly hypothetical practical scenario fills the basic slot. Scenario answers are separated from declarations of work and excluded from work-evidence coverage and optional-test triggers. Missing evidence remains a report gap.
