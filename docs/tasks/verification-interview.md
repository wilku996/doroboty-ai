## Pilot procedure

1. Use the synthetic Anna / AI Product Manager application. Complete the interview, attach the report, submit, then select the BrightLabs recruiter. Let a recruiter unfamiliar with AI read the report without coaching.
2. Ask the recruiter to describe Anna's own contribution, show the passage supporting that interpretation, explain why it matters for the published role, identify an unresolved fact and choose a next step using one specific finding.
3. A role expert checks the explanation against the saved CV, offer and replies. Count a reader once in the numerator only when their interpretation, stated limitation and use of specific evidence are all correct. The denominator includes every participating recruiter who read the report, including those who cannot explain it.
4. Record the reader's anonymous session identifier, interpretation errors, unsupported statements, corrections, review minutes and cost. Record the agreed review rate and currency beside the cost calculation. Report the observed numerator and denominator together. The review form stores the expert's corrections and cumulative review time on the local application.
